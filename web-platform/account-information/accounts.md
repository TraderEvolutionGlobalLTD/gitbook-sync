# Accounts

Accounts panel shows basic details about all accounts that are available for a user.

To open Accounts panel, select Account -> Accounts in the header of VelocityTrader Web platform:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1454).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The Accounts panel is also available in Detailed view in order to show the detailed information in rows. You can see details about any of your accounts or about all together. Hover over the account name on the Current equity section (Balance & Projected balance) located in the header of the Web platform in order to open this panel.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1455).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

By clicking on the Account Link button <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot_8 (2).png" alt="" data-size="original">, located at the Accounts widget, a user can link panels by account. The panels will display data related only to selected account:

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (73).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Please, read more about Account linking [here](../getting-started/arranging-panels.md).

**Displaying totals considering account currency**

The totals are calculated taking into account the account currency according to the filtering results:

* If the filtered entries belong to accounts with the same currency, then the totals are displayed in the account currency.
* If the filtered entries belong to accounts with different currencies, then the totals are displayed in the server currency.
